# Limits and Colimits

## Cones and Cocones

**Cone over F: J → C**: Object X with compatible morphisms to F(j)
**Cocone under F: J → C**: Object X with compatible morphisms from F(j)

## Limit = Terminal Cone

**Lim F**: Universal cone with unique morphisms from any other cone

## Colimit = Initial Cocone

**Colim F**: Universal cocone with unique morphisms to any other cocone

## 15 Examples

1. **Product**: Limit of discrete diagram
2. **Coproduct**: Colimit of discrete
3. **Equalizer**: Limit of parallel pair
4. **Coequalizer**: Colimit of parallel  
5. **Pullback**: Limit of span diagram
6. **Pushout**: Colimit of cospan
7. **Kernel**: Equalizer with zero
8. **Cokernel**: Coequalizer with zero
9. **Terminal object**: Limit of empty diagram
10. **Initial object**: Colimit of empty
11. **Inverse limit**: Limit of inverse system
12. **Direct limit**: Colimit of direct system
13. **Fiber product**: Pullback  
14. **Amalgamated free product**: Pushout
15. **Projective/Inductive limits**

## Preservation

**Right adjoints preserve limits**
**Left adjoints preserve colimits**

Key property for understanding adjunctions!

## Complete/Cocomplete Categories

- **Complete**: Has all limits  
- **Cocomplete**: Has all colimits
- **Bicomplete**: Both

Examples: Set, Top, Grp, Vect, Presheaves (all bicomplete)

## Completeness Theorem

**Theorem**: C is complete iff has products and equalizers
**Dual**: C is cocomplete iff has coproducts and coequalizers

(Other limits/colimits build from these)

## Universal Properties

Limits/colimits characterized by universal properties
Foundation for categorical algebra and Kan extensions
